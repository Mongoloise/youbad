Everything in one place

No more uploading the same video twice: YouBad replaces the back-and-forth between YouTube Studio and the TikTok app.

Import your videos

Add a video from your computer (MP4, MOV or WebM). It is stored in the library of the account you choose.

Several channels

Each account groups one YouTube channel and one TikTok account, with its own videos and statistics.

Post to both at once

Title and description for YouTube, caption for TikTok, visibility chosen by you, then one click publishes to both.

Statistics

Subscribers, views and the performance of your latest YouTube videos, for every account, on one dashboard.

Video library

Every video with its status — draft, ready, scheduled, published — and its publication history.

Local and private

Videos, settings and access tokens stay on your computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than YouTube and TikTok.

How it works

From your video file to two published posts, in four steps.

1

Create an account

In “My accounts”, create an account and link its YouTube channel and its TikTok account through the official sign-in pages of Google and TikTok.

2

Import a video

Choose a video from your computer. YouBad shows a preview and stores it in the account's library.

3

Write and choose

Write the YouTube title and description and the TikTok caption, then choose the visibility, the interaction settings and the commercial content disclosure.

4

Publish to both

Confirm the post: YouBad uploads the video to YouTube and TikTok at the same time, shows the progress, then the statistics on the dashboard.

TikTok integration

YouBad uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API (Direct Post).

1

Sign in with TikTok

The user clicks “Link the TikTok account”. The TikTok authorization page opens in the browser and lists the permissions requested. Nothing is linked until the user approves.

2

Account shown in the app

YouBad displays the linked account's name and avatar, so the user always knows which account will post.

3

Post with explicit choices

Before each post, the user sees the creator's nickname, writes the caption, picks the privacy level from the options allowed by TikTok, sets comments, Duet and Stitch, declares commercial content if any, and confirms.

4

Status and statistics

After posting, the user sees the upload progress and is told the video may take a few minutes to be processed and appear on their profile.
