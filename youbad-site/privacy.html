Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

1. Who uses YouBad

YouBad is a personal desktop application used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels and TikTok accounts. It does not collect data from any other person.

2. Data received

When a TikTok account is connected, YouBad receives: the account's display name, username and avatar; account statistics (follower, following, like and video counts); the list of the account's videos with their view, like, comment and share counts; and access tokens allowing YouBad to publish videos on behalf of the owner.

When a YouTube channel is connected, YouBad receives the channel name, its statistics, the list of its recent videos with their statistics, and access tokens allowing YouBad to upload videos.

3. How the data is used

This data is used only to display statistics to the owner inside the application and to publish the owner's videos to the owner's own accounts.

4. Storage and sharing

All data and access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. They are never sold, shared with or transferred to third parties, and are not used for advertising.

5. Deletion

Disconnecting an account in YouBad deletes its stored tokens. Access can also be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Apps and services permissions) or in the Google account permissions page (https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

6. Contact

For any question about this policy, contact the owner at the email address associated with the YouBad developer account.
