Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

1. Summary

YouBad runs on your computer. It has no server and no user account of its own. Your videos, your settings and the access tokens of your YouTube and TikTok accounts are stored only on your computer and are only sent to YouTube and TikTok.

2. Data received from TikTok

When you link a TikTok account, YouBad receives: your display name and avatar; your account statistics (follower, following, like and video counts); your nickname, username and posting settings (available privacy levels, interaction settings, maximum video length), read each time you open the post window; the list of your recent videos with their view, like, comment and share counts; and access tokens allowing YouBad to publish the videos you confirm.

3. Data received from YouTube

When you link a YouTube channel, YouBad receives the channel name, its statistics, the list of its recent videos with their statistics, and access tokens allowing YouBad to upload the videos you confirm.

4. How the data is used

This data is used only to show you which account will post, to show your statistics inside the app, and to publish the videos you choose, with the settings you choose.

5. Sharing

YouBad does not sell, rent or share your data with anyone, does not use it for advertising and does not send it to any server other than YouTube and TikTok.

6. Deleting your data

Disconnecting an account in YouBad deletes its stored tokens. Uninstalling YouBad and deleting its folder removes all its data. You can also revoke access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Apps and services permissions) or on your Google account permissions page.

7. Contact

For any question about this policy, open an issue on the YouBad GitHub page.
