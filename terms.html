1. The application

YouBad is a desktop application for Windows that lets creators import their own videos and publish them to their own YouTube channel and TikTok account at the same time. YouBad is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by TikTok or YouTube.

2. Your accounts

To publish, you link your YouTube channel and your TikTok account through the official sign-in pages of Google and TikTok. By linking an account, you authorize YouBad to read basic account information and statistics and to publish the videos you choose. You can disconnect an account at any time in YouBad, and revoke access in your Google or TikTok account settings.

3. Your content

You only publish content you own or have the right to publish. You are responsible for the videos, titles, descriptions and captions you post. Content must respect the YouTube Terms of Service, the TikTok Terms of Service and their Community Guidelines.

Nothing is published without your action: every post is reviewed and confirmed by you. YouBad does not add watermarks, logos or promotional content to your videos.

YouBad uses the YouTube Data API and the TikTok API. By using YouBad you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, the Google Privacy Policy and the TikTok Terms of Service. When posting to TikTok you also agree to TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation and, for branded content, TikTok's Branded Content Policy.
